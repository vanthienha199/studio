How I work

Before I start, we write down what done looks like and how we will check it. Then I work in small steps you can see: a short update most days, and every change as its own commit with a plain sentence about what it does and why. The parts that matter get tests, so a later change cannot quietly break them. At the end you own all of it, the code, the accounts and a short guide, and you can read the whole history the way you would read a diary of the job.
